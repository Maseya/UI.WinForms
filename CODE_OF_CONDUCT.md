# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of age,
background, experience level, identity, or personal characteristics.

We pledge to make participation in this project a positive experience for
everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Accepting differing opinions and perspectives.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Showing empathy toward other participants.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or threats.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub
repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other communication
channels associated with the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code
of Conduct.

Behavior that violates these standards may result in removal of comments or
contributions, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces.
